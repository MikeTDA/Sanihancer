HOW TO USE

5 Steps to a Sanihancer-Clean Environment

1

Remove All Animals

Move birds or livestock to one side or out of the area before applying — or use with animals present for routine maintenance.

2

Clear All Waste & Debris

Scrape out all manure, litter and nesting material. Disinfectants are inactivated by dirt — clean surfaces get full results.

3

Wash With Soap & Water

Scrub all surfaces — cage bars, feeders, drinkers, walls and floors — with soap and water before applying.

4

Apply Sanihancer™

For poultry: load into a low-pressure garden sprayer. 2 litres covers 3–5 cages. For septic: pour directly into toilet and flush. Saturate all surfaces completely.

5

Wait & Return

Let sit 10–30 minutes until surfaces are completely dry. Return animals to a clean, safe, odor-free environment.

💡 Pro Tip: Repeat weekly — or at minimum twice per month — for continuous protection and maximum results.
